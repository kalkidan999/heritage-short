# Experiments

| Hypothesis | Change | Observed result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A character sheet can preserve Kaldi's appearance | Conditioned the opening still on the revised wardrobe sheet | Opening retained the tan shirt, dark shorts reference, and olive scarf where visible. |
| Pitch shifting can give Kaldi a younger voice without speeding him up | Applied pitch shifting to copies of the six recordings | The clips fit their original slots and the radio cut remained 76 seconds. |
| Local speech animation can work without a Kaggle GPU | Tested a three-second SadTalker render on CPU | Creator confirmed that the mouth followed the speech; full opening then rendered. |
| Lowering music alone will leave speech intelligible | Mixed the reference track quietly under the existing voice clips | Creator reported hearing music but not narration. |
| Raising quiet voice clips before mixing will improve separation | Normalized voice peaks, reduced music gain, and ducked music under speech | Opening mix measured -19.6 dB mean and -4.8 dB peak in both exports; creator approved the revised mix. |
